Understand

WH Questions: review

WHEN → time

Usamos WHEN para perguntar sobre tempo.

  • When do you go to school?
  • When do you play with your animals?
  • When does Oakley go to school? → He goes to school in the morning.
  • I go to school at 13:00. Sometimes, I go at 14:00.

WHY → reason

Usamos WHY para perguntar sobre uma razão ou motivo.

  • Why do we have to go to school?
  • Why are you happy today?
  • Why does Oakley go to school? → Because he wants to learn!
  • Because I got an A on the Math test.

WHAT → things / information

Usamos WHAT para perguntar sobre coisas ou informações.

  • What is a Dachshund? → It is a dog breed.
  • What animal is Oakley?
  • What do you like to do on weekends?
  • What is your favorite subject?
Understand

WHERE and WOULD

WHERE → place

Usamos WHERE para perguntar sobre lugares.

  • Where is Oakley at? → Oakley is at school.
  • Where is the party? → The party is at my house.
  • Where are you going?
  • Where is your house? → My house is near the beach.
  • Where do you play after school?
  • Where do you like to go with your best friends?

WOULD → polite / choices

Usamos WOULD para perguntas educadas e para falar de escolhas ou possibilidades.

  • Would you adopt a Dachshund?
  • Would you like to have a Dachshund dog? → Yes, I would.
  • Would you travel to Japan?
  • Would you wear a vampire costume?

WOULD + base verb (forma base do verbo, sem -s).

I like dogs. → Would you like dogs?
He plays games. → Would he play games?
She helps you. → Would she help you?
They go to school. → Would they go to school?

I like dogs. = Eu gosto de cachorros.
I would like dogs. = Eu gostaria de cachorros.
Would I like dogs? = Eu gostaria de cachorros?
